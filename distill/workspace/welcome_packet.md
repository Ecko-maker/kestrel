# Welcome to Greenspoke

Greenspoke is a community garden and bike co-op. Members get a garden plot when one is free,
use of the repair shed during workshops, and member rates on bike rentals.

Membership: $35 a year. Pay Tomasz (our treasurer) or at any workshop.
Shed code: ask Aiko in person. We never send it by email.
Questions: ines@greenspoke.example
